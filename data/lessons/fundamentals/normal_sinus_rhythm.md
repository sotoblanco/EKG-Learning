# Normal Sinus Rhythm (NSR)

Normal Sinus Rhythm is the standard rhythm of a healthy heart, originating from the SA node.

> [!NOTE]
> **Criteria for NSR:**
> - **Rate:** 60 - 100 bpm
> - **Rhythm:** Regular
> - **P waves:** Present, upright in II, and precede every QRS complex
> - **PR Interval:** Normal (0.12 - 0.20s)

Understanding NSR is the baseline for identifying all other arrhythmias.
