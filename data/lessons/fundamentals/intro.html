EKG Fundamentals: Introduction

Welcome to the Fundamentals module. The electrocardiogram (EKG) is one of the most useful diagnostic tools in clinical medicine.

Core Concept: The EKG records the electrical activity of the heart, which precedes its mechanical contraction.

Key Learning Objectives

  • Understand the Standard EKG Grid (Time vs. Voltage).
  • Identify the 12 Standard Leads and their anatomical views.
  • Recognize the components of a Normal EKG Waveform (P, QRS, T).

In the following cases, you will apply these basic principles to identify normal sinus rhythm and common technical errors like lead reversal.
